Support

For all games published by Stenumedia

Something broken, confusing, or missing? Write to us and a real person will answer.

Contact

Use Contact developer in the game's Settings. It tells us which game and player you are, and your game version and device model, so we can find the problem faster. You can also email players@stenumedia.com.

We usually reply within a few days.

What to include

  • Which game you are playing
  • What you expected and what happened instead
  • Your device and its operating system version
  • A screenshot, if you have one

Common questions

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. Use Delete my account in the game. If you no longer have the game installed, email us with the game name, your player name and your friend code if the game has one. We may ask you to confirm something only the owner of the account would know before we delete anything. Your data is deleted within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I add a friend?

In games with friends, open Friends, share your friend code, or paste theirs and tap Add.

I got a new phone. Is my progress gone?

Write to us, from the old phone if you still have it, and we will help you restore your progress where we can.

The game says there is maintenance or an update is required.

We take the servers down briefly when we update them. Try again in a few minutes. If a new version is required, update the game from your device's app store.

I did not get something I bought, or I want a refund.

Restart the game first, as a purchase can take a moment to arrive. If it is still missing, write to us with the receipt from the app store. Refunds are handled by the app store you bought from.

How do I turn notifications off?

Turn them off in the game's Settings, or in your device's settings for the game.

Someone is bothering me, or I saw something that should not be in a game.

Report the content or block the player in the game. You can also tell us the player's name or friend code and what happened. We review every report and act on it.
