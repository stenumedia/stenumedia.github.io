Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy covers every game published by Stenumedia Oy ("we", "us") that links to it, and this website. We collect only what the games need to work, and we do not sell your data.

Our games do not all have the same features. Where this policy says "may", it applies only to games that have that feature and only if you use it.

Who is responsible

Stenumedia Oy · Business ID 2473457-8 · P.O. Box 1000, 01741 Vantaa, Finland. Contact: players@stenumedia.com. Stenumedia Oy is the data controller.

What we collect

Depending on the game and the features you use, we may collect:

  • Player profile. The name you choose and other profile details you set, such as an avatar. Other players can see these.
  • Player ID. A random ID the game creates for you, so that your progress stays yours. It does not contain your name, but it identifies your player (a pseudonymous ID).
  • Platform account. If you sign in or link your player through a platform service, such as Game Center, Google Play Games or Sign in with Apple, an identifier for that account and the details that service shares with us. We never receive your password.
  • Friends. Your friend code, your friend list, and requests and invitations you send or receive.
  • Game data. Your progress, results, rankings, items and settings, and a record of the games you have played.
  • Content you create. Things you make or send in a game, such as drawings, guesses, text, messages and reactions, and reports you make about other players or their content.
  • Purchases. If you buy something, what you bought and when, and the receipt from the app store so we can deliver it. The app store handles the payment. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Notification token. If you allow notifications, a token from your device's platform so we can send them.
  • Technical data. Your IP address, game version, device model, operating system version, language and region, and error and crash logs.
  • Usage data. How the game is used, such as which features and modes are played and for how long.
  • Messages to us. What you send through Contact developer or by email, including your email address if you write by email.

The games do not ask for your real name, phone number, contacts or precise location. If a game needs access to something on your device, such as your photos, camera or microphone, your device asks you first and we use the access only for the feature you chose.

Our games do not show third-party advertising and do not track you across other companies' apps and websites. If that ever changes for a game, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • Running the games (player profile, player ID, platform account, friends, game data, content you create, technical data): to provide the game and the features you chose to use. Legal basis: performing our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Purchases: to deliver what you bought and to restore it on a new device. Legal basis: performing our contract with you, and our legal obligations for accounting.
  • Notifications (notification token): to send notifications you have allowed. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Security, fair play and moderation (technical data, game data, content you create, reports): to keep the games secure and fair, to prevent cheating and abuse, and to act on reports. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Fixing and improving the games (technical data, usage data): to find and fix problems and to understand what to improve. Legal basis: our legitimate interest.
  • Answering your messages: Legal basis: our legitimate interest in helping you, or the contract with you where your question is about the game.
  • Legal obligations, such as accounting and responding to authorities: Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c).

You do not have to give us any data beyond what the game itself asks for, but a player ID, a player name and game data are needed to play online. Notifications are optional.

We may use automatic checks to detect cheating and abuse. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you by automated means alone. If you think your player was restricted by mistake, write to us and a person will look at it.

What other players see

Other players may see your player profile, your friend code when you share it, whether you are online or in a game, your results and rankings, and content you create or send in a game they are part of or that you choose to share. Do not put personal information in your name or in anything you create.

Some players in a game may be controlled by the computer. A computer-controlled player is not a real person and no personal data is collected about it.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that host our servers, deliver notifications and email, and help us find errors. They handle data only on our behalf and under contract.
  • App stores and platforms, such as Apple and Google, when you download a game, buy something or use their sign-in or game services. They handle that data under their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it or it is needed to protect our rights or other people's safety.
  • A new owner, if a game or our business is transferred. The new owner must follow this policy, and we will tell you before your data is used differently.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Player data (profile, player ID, platform account, friends, game data, content you create): kept until you delete your account. If you delete your account or ask us to delete your data, it is deleted within 30 days.
  • Content shown only during a game, such as drawings and guesses in a round: kept only as long as the game needs it, unless it has been reported, in which case we keep it until the report is handled.
  • Notification token: deleted when you turn notifications off, when the platform tells us it is no longer valid, or with your player data.
  • Server logs and usage data (IP address, technical data, error logs): 30 days. After that we keep only statistics that cannot be linked to you.
  • Backups. Deleted data may remain in our encrypted backups for a limited time, until those backups are replaced. We do not use it for anything during that time.
  • Messages to us: kept for as long as needed to handle the matter and any follow-up.
  • Purchase and accounting records that we must keep by law are kept for the legally required period.
  • Records of a suspension or removal for breaking our terms are kept for as long as the suspension or removal is in force, so that a removed player cannot simply return. They hold only the IDs needed for that and the reason.

Where it is stored

Some of our service providers, and the platforms that deliver notifications, may handle data outside the European Economic Area. When they do, the transfer is protected by a European Commission adequacy decision or by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Write to us if you want to know more about these safeguards.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted when it travels between the game and our servers, access to our servers is limited to those who need it, and we keep only the data described in this policy. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have wrong data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict how we use your data while a request is being handled;
  • receive the data you gave us in a portable format;
  • object to processing that is based on our legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example for notifications, which you can also turn off in your device settings. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before.

These rights come from the GDPR, and we give them to all players wherever they live. To delete your account and its data, use Delete my account in the game. For anything else, use Contact developer in the game's Settings, or email players@stenumedia.com. We answer within 30 days. We may need to confirm that the player is yours first. You may also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi) or to the authority in your own country.

Children

The games are not meant for children under 13, or under the age at which the law of your country lets you agree to the use of your data without a parent, if that is higher. We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

We update this policy when the games or the law change. We will update the date above and, if a change affects how your data is used in a way that matters, tell you in the game before it takes effect.
